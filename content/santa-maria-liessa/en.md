On the valley floor in the municipality of Grimacco, the village of Liessa (Liesa in Slovene) is recognisable by the domed bell tower of the church of Santa Maria del Buonconsiglio (Our Lady of Good Counsel), the parish church and the more important of the municipality's two churches.

## History

A first chapel was built in Liessa in 1687. In the 19th century it was replaced by a larger church that incorporated its apse: the sources give either 1830 or 1870. The new church was consecrated in 1936 by Giuseppe Nogara, Archbishop of Udine. Damaged by the 1976 earthquake, it was restored in 1984–1985.

## What to see

On the façade, in a niche, is a **mosaic of Saint Barbara**, a gift from the municipality's emigrants. The **bronze door**, from 1995, is the work of the sculptor Giorgio Benedetti and depicts Saints Hermagoras and Paulinus. Beside the church rises the square bell tower, with a twin window on each side of the belfry and a copper dome.

Inside there is a single nave with side chapels. The vault bears a late 19th-century fresco of the Virgin among the clouds, surrounded by angels. Note also the stained-glass windows by master Kregar and an organ built in 1841 by Valentino Zanini.

## Slovene song

Every November, Liessa hosts the **Senjam beneške piesmi**, the Slovene song festival of the Natisone Valleys, organised by the Rečan cultural association: new songs by authors from Benečija, with lyrics in the local Slovene dialect. Check dates and programme each year.
