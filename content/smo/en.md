**SMO** (*Slovensko multimedialno okno*, "Slovene multimedia window") is a museum in San Pietro al Natisone (Špietar) devoted to the cultural landscape stretching from the Julian Alps to the sea, from Mangart to the Gulf of Trieste, and to the Slovene communities who live there. It calls itself a "landscape and narrative museum": rather than showing collections of objects, it tells stories through sound, images and voices.

## The museum

SMO was created in 2013, within the European project JezikLingua, as part of the Institute for Slovene Culture (ISK), in the Slovene cultural centre in San Pietro. It was conceived, designed and coordinated by the architect Donatella Ruttar and co-financed by the Italy–Slovenia cross-border cooperation programme 2007–2013. It belongs to the *Mi SMO Tu* museum network.

## The installations

The visit is made up of eight multimedia installations:

- **Meja / Border**: a multimedia sculpture on a relief model shows how the region's borders have changed from the 6th century to today;
- **Spomini iz besed / Voice memories**: a chest of voices and memories in the remarkably rich local dialects;
- **Glasbena krajina / Musical landscape**: a large interactive musical installation on seven cultural landscapes;
- **Govoreča knjižnica / Speaking library**: touch a book and its author, one of the leading writers of the border area, appears on screen;
- **Ilustrirana zgodovina / Illustrated history**: videos, including *Jezik v času*, the history of written Slovene illustrated by Cosimo Miorelli;
- **Kulturne krajine / Cultural landscapes**: a triptych of screens from Tarvisio to Trieste, the Natisone Valleys included;
- **Zvočne razglednice / Audio postcards**: the soundscape of the area;
- **Atlas**: an interactive map with an atlas of place names.

## Good to know

The museum is at Via Alpe Adria 73, San Pietro al Natisone. The official website gives daily opening from 10:00 to 13:00 and 14:30 to 17:30, with exceptions (in August, for example): check opening times and bookings at [smo-center.eu](https://www.smo-center.eu/). The museum also runs activities for schools. CAI trail 749 to the Matajur and trail 776 to Cividale start from San Pietro.
