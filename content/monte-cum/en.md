title: Monte Cum / Hum and the Great War trail
description: Monte Cum (Hum, 912 m) above Tribil Superiore: Italy's last line in 1917, and a loop past trenches, caves and artillery positions.
---
Monte Cum (Hum in Slovene) rises to 912 metres between Tribil Superiore (Gorenji Tarbij), in the municipality of Stregna, and San Volfango (Svet Štoublank), in the municipality of Drenchia, close to the Slovenian border. Wooded all over, it is easy to spot from the valleys and the plain thanks to its truncated-pyramid shape, standing alone with no other peaks nearby. The name comes from the Slovene word *holm*, "hill", which over time became *hum*.

## The last line of 1917

During the First World War, the last defensive line of the Italian 2nd Army ran over Monte Cum and the surrounding heights, almost as far as Cividale: it was meant to stop the enemy before the Friulian plain in case of retreat. Along the ridge between the Judrio and Erbezzo valleys a military road was built from Castelmonte to the Solarie pass, with trenches, huts and caves dug for shelter and communication; enemy artillery targeted it from the first days of 1916.

In the Battle of Caporetto the summit was among the hardest-hit positions. Defended by the Elba Brigade, it was taken by the 8th Leib-Grenadier Regiment at 11 a.m. on 26 October 1917, while the surviving Italian units fell back towards Castelmonte and Monte Spik. Tunnels and gun emplacements can still be seen.

## The Great War trail

Since 2016 a waymarked loop, the **Great War trail of Monte Cum** (*Sentiero Hum* on maps), starts from Tribil Superiore, climbs the southern ridge to the summit, descends to the hamlet of Rucchin and returns to the start through the woods of the northern ridge. Along the way you will find:

- caves and platforms dug to shelter and position artillery;
- the 2nd Army's trenches and craters left by Austro-Hungarian shells;
- remains of barracks and depots for war materiel;
- anti-aircraft artillery positions.

Allow about three and a half hours. A short variant climbs from the municipal road to CAI trail 747.

**CAI trail 747**, part of the Sentiero Italia and of the Natisone Valleys high route, also crosses the summit; remains of fortifications and trenches are still visible along it.

## Good to know

Tribil Superiore, at 642 metres, is the highest village in the municipality of Stregna; it has the Gothic church of San Giovanni Battista, from the 16th century. On the night of 23 June the *kries*, the midsummer bonfire of Saint John, is lit there. Take care near the war caves and tunnels.
