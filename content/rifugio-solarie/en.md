On the southern slope of Mount Kolovrat (Colovrat), in the municipality of Drenchia, the **Solarie hut** stands at 956 metres beside the Solarie pass, a former border crossing linking the Cosizza valley with the Slovenian village of Volzana and the Soča (Isonzo) valley. Half an hour higher up the ridge is the **Giuseppe Zanuso bivouac** (1116 m). Together they are the gateway to the Kolovrat ridge and its First World War sites.

## The Great War on the Kolovrat

During the First World War the Kolovrat ridge was the last line of defence of the Italian 2nd Army, with an extensive system of trenches, tunnels and fortifications. On the morning of 24 October 1917, at the start of the Battle of Caporetto, it came under heavy bombardment, including gas shells, which caused very many casualties among soldiers and civilians. Then German companies under Lieutenant Erwin Rommel took the summits in a surprise attack and pressed on towards the Matajur.

Trenches, tunnels cut into the rock, communication trenches and remains of concrete fortifications can still be seen. In the Na Gradu Klabuk area (Monte Poclabuz) a **cross-border open-air museum** has been created, with restored trenches, tunnels and positions. Near the pass, a memorial stone honours **Riccardo Giusto**, regarded as the first Italian soldier killed in the war: a ceremony is held there every year on the first Sunday of June.

## The Zanuso bivouac

In January 1929 the Alpine soldier Giuseppe Zanuso, born in Valdagno in 1908, died at this spot, caught in a violent snowstorm. A memorial stone keeps his memory; opposite it, in 1992, the bivouac bearing his name was built. It is a small stone shelter with a viewing terrace, always open and unstaffed, but no longer equipped with a stove or crockery: treat it as a shelter for a short stop. From the Solarie pass it takes about 30 minutes along a farm road, paved at first and then unsurfaced. A memorial ceremony is held on the first Sunday of September.

## Trails

The trails of the **Natisone Valleys high route** (Sentiero Italia, CAI 745, 746 and 747) and the Alpe Adria Trail pass the hut. Possible traverses include:

- to the Pelizzo hut below the Matajur along the high route: about 5½ hours;
- along the Kolovrat ridge from Rucchin to Topolò: about 13 km, 6 hours;
- on trail 746, from Topolò via Lase, the Kolovrat, Monte Nagnoj and the Zanuso bivouac to the hut: about 4 hours.

## Good to know

The hut can also be reached by car, from Clodig or from Castelmonte and Tribil. Opening days and hours vary with the seasons: phone before setting out.
